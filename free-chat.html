Your tutor

Bilingual. Paris since 2011. Surprisingly local.

I'm Dan. I moved to France at 18 with an A-level in French, thoroughly convinced I had it sorted. Then I landed a job at Disneyland Paris and asked for a travel pass in my broadest English accent. The reply came back in fast Parisian French, and I froze. I didn't want to speak French again for about six weeks.

What got me through it was people, not a course: French friends who coached me one conversation at a time, and colleagues who spoke to me slowly and clearly until my accent settled. I went on to become Disney's professional voice actor for a well-known little blue alien, with the studio's own vocal coaching sharpening my French further still.

My whole family's in theatre, but my dad encouraged me to have another skill, so I trained in finance and spent several years as a Financial Analyst at Disneyland Paris. Same organisation, completely different French. I can talk you through ordering a glass of wine or a corporate finance meeting. People assume the finance is the hard part. Come sit in a pub with me and my mates and find out which one actually is.
