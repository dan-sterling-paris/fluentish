Your tutor

Bilingual. Paris since 2011. Surprisingly local.

I'm Dan. I moved to France at 18 with an A-level in French, convinced I had it sorted. I landed a job at Disneyland Paris, asked for a travel pass in my broadest English accent, and got a reply in fast Parisian French that made me freeze. I didn't speak French again for six weeks.

People got me through it, not a course: French friends who coached me one conversation at a time, colleagues who spoke slowly and clearly until my accent settled. I went on to become Disney's voice actor for a well-known little blue alien, with the studio's own vocal coaching sharpening my French further.

My whole family's in theatre, but my dad encouraged me to have another skill, so I trained in finance and spent several years as a Financial Analyst at Disneyland Paris. Same organisation, completely different French. I can talk you through ordering a glass of wine or a corporate finance meeting. People assume the finance is the hard part. Come sit in a pub with me and my mates and find out which one actually is.
