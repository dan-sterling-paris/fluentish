Still got questions?

Do I pay per session or upfront?

Per session. No upfront blocks, no "buy ten and save" pressure, no money changing hands until we've had the free chat and you've decided to go ahead. Payment details are agreed at the start of our first proper session.

What's the cancellation policy?

Life happens and I'm not unreasonable. 24 hours' notice to reschedule is what I ask. Cancellations with less notice may be charged. We'll agree the specifics before we start. I hold up my end too: I don't cancel, I don't reschedule without good reason, and I'm not late.

Is this online or in person?

Online sessions via video call are the default, and available to anyone in the UK. In-person sessions in Greater Manchester are also available if you're local. We'll sort out which works best on the free call.

How do I know if a spot is still available?

Book the free chat and find out. If I'm at capacity, I'll tell you immediately and won't waste your time. If there's a spot, we talk. Simple as that.
