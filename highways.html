M1Word Highways

Each phrase is a town and each sentence is a numbered route. Every time the text moves from one town to the next, that road gets used, and roads travelled again and again widen into highways. The sat-nav follows the exact route of the text and only announces a turn where the text genuinely repeats itself and could have gone another way.

Press Play or drag the slider to start.
StartStart
↑

The pink road is the route the text takes. Other roads only appear where the text repeats the same run of phrases somewhere else, using the longest repeat it can find, so every option really happens in the text. Solid roads have been travelled already; dashed ones come up later. Click another road to leave the route: the sat-nav recalculates and carries on from where the text takes that road.

Directions

The whole route, turn by turn. Like a real sat-nav it stays quiet while the text follows the main road, and speaks up at forks (equally used roads) and turns (where the text leaves a busier road). Click a step to go there.

Transcript

Underlines show where the text was cut into towns. Click any town to jump there.
