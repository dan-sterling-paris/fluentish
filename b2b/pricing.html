Common questions

Is this legal translation?

No. I am not a certified legal translator and this is not certified translation. What I provide is comprehension, context, and business-level communication support. For documents requiring certified translation — court submissions, immigration paperwork, officially notarised contracts — you'll need a qualified legal translator. For everything else in normal business life, this covers it.

How do I contact you for urgent requests?

We'll agree a contact method on the discovery call — typically email, with a response commitment of same-day for standard requests. For genuinely urgent situations we'll agree a direct contact route. I'm based in Greater Manchester and Paris, and I work across both time zones.

What if I need more than 4 hours in a month?

If you have a particularly busy month, we'll flag it early and agree how to handle it — either a one-off additional fee or a move to a larger retainer scope. Custom retainers from £1,000/month are available for businesses with higher ongoing French language demands. We'll work out what makes sense on the discovery call.

Can you join calls on short notice?

48 hours' notice is the standard for scheduled calls. I understand that business doesn't always cooperate with that. For calls that come up quickly, contact me directly and I'll do what I can. We'll discuss expectations on the discovery call so there are no surprises.

How does cancellation work?

30 days' notice to end the retainer. No long-term lock-in. The goal is for the retainer to be worth more to your business than it costs — if that's not the case, you should be able to leave without a fight.

Do you only work with Manchester businesses?

Greater Manchester is the primary focus, but the async model means I can work with businesses anywhere in the UK. If you're outside Manchester, the only difference is that in-person meetings aren't available — everything else works the same way. Get in touch and we'll work out if it's a fit.
